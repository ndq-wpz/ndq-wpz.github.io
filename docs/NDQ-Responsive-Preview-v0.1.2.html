Manufacturing Accounting & Month-End Financial Controls

Reconstructed a January close from source documents to the trial balance and preliminary financial statements.

9/9 statement checks passed · 10 findings

Explore case studyClose case study
Objective
Trace a manufacturing order and a purchase-to-pay cycle, then test whether the January ledger supports the reported balances.
Method
Followed material issues, work in progress, goods receipts, invoices and payments. Reconciled account movements in Power Query and tied the closing trial balance to the preliminary statements.
Finding
All 67 accounts and nine statement checks tied. A credit balance in manufacturing clearing and a work order closed in February still warranted investigation.
Recommended action
Review clearing entries against the work-order history and obtain independent subledger and bank reconciliations before sign-off.

Jenoss — E-commerce Sales Reconstruction & Cost Master Review

Rebuilt Amazon commercial sales in three currencies and identified where product costs were missing or estimated.

2,587 source lines · 2,206 shipped

Explore case studyClose case study
Objective
Identify which shipped lines generated commercial sales and how many units support product-cost analysis.
Method
Consolidated six Amazon Orders files in Power Query. Isolated commercial, Vine, replacement and unknown zero-price lines before joining the SKU and cost masters.
Finding
All 65 commercial SKUs mapped. The cost review identified 53 SKUs with master costs, two with estimates and ten with missing costs.
Recommended action
Prioritize the five active zero-cost internal SKU keys. Verify cost currency, basis and effective dates before reporting product margins.
